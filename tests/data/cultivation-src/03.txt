The Reed Contest

Every winter the Silver Reed Sect held a contest on the frozen pond, and every disciple who wished could stand on the ice and spar until one of them fell through or gave up. Tamsin entered because Elder Kovic Brannoch told her she was not ready, which she took as an invitation.

She won her first bout against a nervous boy who slipped before she touched him, and her second against a girl twice her size who fought very well and apologised every time she struck. The third bout was against Dace Mullery.

Dace did not hold back. The roots of his cultivation ran through the ice beneath him, and wherever she stepped the surface cracked and tried to swallow her boots. She lost the bout in the time it took the reeds to bow twice, and she lay on the ice afterwards with her ears ringing.

But something had broken loose while she fought. The mist in her belly had been pressed so hard against her ribs that it had poured into a space she had not known was there.

Kovic knelt beside her on the ice and took her wrist before she could sit up.

"Tamsin Vey has reached the ninth layer of Mist Gathering," he said, loud enough for the watching disciples to hear. "She lost the bout and found the last layer of the first realm. That is a better trade than winning."

Dace offered her his hand, and she took it. "Next winter," he said.

"Next spring," she said, and he laughed and did not argue.

The ice groaned under them both as they walked back to the bank. Behind them the reeds were still bent flat where the contest had pressed them, and the other disciples were already telling one another, in voices too low for Kovic to hear, that the girl who counted frogs had climbed six layers in a single year. Tamsin wrote the third line in her copybook that night, and her hand shook so much that the ink ran.
